# Third-party notices

Practice Companion loads nothing from a third-party origin at runtime: there
are no remote fonts, analytics, players, or scripts. Icons are drawn inline in
`src/components/Icon.tsx`, PWA icons are generated by
`scripts/generate-icons.mjs`, the metronome is synthesised with Web Audio, and
charts are plain HTML and CSS.
